Kansen instrument resynthesis references

The app contains measured oscillator frequencies, spectral envelopes, upper-mode phase evolution and noise-band
levels derived from these references. It does not contain or play their waveform
recordings. Analysis includes mono conversion, onset trimming, spectral analysis
and retuning to the app's reference pitch. Synthetic output uses new oscillators
and stochastic noise. Measurement script: tools/analyze_instruments.py.

Harmonium: Donya Quick, "Harmonium Samples - All Keys and Drones", recorded at
Yale University's Euterpea Studio. Original: CC0 1.0.
https://freesound.org/people/donyaquick/sounds/330410/
Twenty-six C3–D5 reference excerpts prepared by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky / tonejs-instruments,
whose distributed samples are CC BY 3.0.
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/tree/master/samples/harmonium

Harmonium notes above D5 use a synthetic extension that preserves the measured
body spectral envelope. Reference input hashes are recorded in sources.json.
