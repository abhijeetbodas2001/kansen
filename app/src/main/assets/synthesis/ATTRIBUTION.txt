Patukarna instrument resynthesis references

The app contains measured oscillator frequencies, spectral envelopes, upper-mode phase evolution and noise-band
levels derived from these references. It does not contain or play their waveform
recordings. Analysis includes mono conversion, onset trimming, spectral analysis
and retuning to the app's reference pitch. Synthetic output uses new oscillators
and stochastic noise. Measurement script: tools/analyze_instruments.py.

https://freesound.org/people/donyaquick/sounds/330410/
